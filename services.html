Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book catering services?

For large weddings and corporate galas with over 100 guests, we recommend booking at least 3 to 6 weeks in advance. For intimate private parties (20–50 guests), 5 to 7 business days notice is usually sufficient depending on kitchen availability.

What areas do you deliver to for food orders?

We offer express dispatch to Lekki Phase 1, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Oniru, Banana Island, and surrounding estates. For orders outside these zones, special dispatch can be scheduled upon request.

Can menu items be customized for dietary preferences?

Absolutely! We cater to specific spice tolerance levels (Mild, Medium, Hot, Extra Hot) and can prepare vegetarian/vegan versions of our soups (such as pure palm-oil vegetable soup without crayfish) upon special instruction.
